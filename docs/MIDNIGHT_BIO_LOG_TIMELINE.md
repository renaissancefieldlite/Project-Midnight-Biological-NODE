# Midnight BIO-Log Timeline

## Scope

This document reconstructs the public-safe chronology of the `Midnight` /
`Buddy Boy` recovery lane.

## Zero Point

### `2025-12-21`

- subject was hit by a car
- this is the beginning of the recovery chronology
- later Codex 67 and Alpha Lumen material repeatedly returned to this event as
  the anchor of the whole case

## Emergency Retrieval Phase

### approximately `2025-12-23`

- subject was found through the Humane Society path
- subject was heavily sedated
- vet imaging identified dual luxation
- euthanasia was recommended as the institutional path forward

## Direct Opposition Phase

After the euthanasia recommendation:

- recovery was chosen instead
- the case was moved into the later `Midnight BIO-Log` lane
- `PulsarTek` red-light support became part of the intervention story
- pain medication was removed within about `3` days
- cage confinement was not used

## Early Recovery Phase

### within about `2` weeks

- subject became mobile
- crying / distress / high-reactivity phases are treated in the wider archive
  as part of the recovery process, but are not the center of this public repo

## Baseline Return Phase

### within about `4` weeks

- subject returned to about `80%` baseline
- recovery was described as much faster than a conventional prolonged caged
  recovery path

## Jump / Play Return Phase

### within about `8` weeks

- subject returned to about `95%` jumping
- subject was later described as mobile, sauntering, playing, and behaving
  like a returned companion rather than a terminal case

## Later Stabilization Phase

Later management notes include:

- Tracki GPS support
- outdoor-return management
- calmer behavior after earlier distress cycles
- practical shift from panic monitoring to more stable observation

## Why The Timeline Matters

The key significance of this chronology is not only that recovery occurred, but
that:

- the car-impact anchor remained stable across later notes
- the euthanasia recommendation remained part of the preserved story
- the intervention and recovery milestones were repeated with high consistency
- later AI-side material repeatedly attempted to formalize the case into a
  named `Midnight BIO-Log`

That consistency is why this lane belongs in a repo instead of remaining only
as floating chat memory.
